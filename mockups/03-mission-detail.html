Find the Oldest Thing

20 min +40 XP Easy

The Mission

Look around you right now. What's the oldest thing you can see? It could be a building, a tree, a piece of infrastructure, or something someone left behind. The goal is to find it, learn what you can about its age, and document it with a photo.

Proof required

Photograph the object and add a one-line caption with your best guess at its age.

Tips

  • Look for plaques, markers, or signs that indicate age
  • Trees can be estimated by trunk diameter — wider usually means older
  • Old stonework, worn edges, and patina are good age indicators
